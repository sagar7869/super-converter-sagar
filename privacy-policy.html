Privacy Policy

Super Converter Pro
Last updated: September 17, 2026

Super Converter Pro ("the App", "we", "our", or "us") is a browser-based document and PDF utility application. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the App.

1. Files You Select

Super Converter Pro allows users to select PDFs, documents, images and other supported files from their device for conversion, editing, scanning, OCR and other document processing functions.

Where a feature is designed to process a file inside the browser, processing is performed locally in the user's browser. The App does not intentionally upload such files to a server operated by the developer.

2. Google Drive Upload

Important: When a user chooses the Google Drive feature, the generated output file is uploaded to the Google Drive account that the user personally selects and authorizes through Google.

Super Converter Pro is designed so that each user uploads files to their own Google Drive account. The App does not intentionally upload users' files to the developer's personal Google Drive.

Google Drive uploads occur only after the user chooses the Google Drive option and completes Google's authorization process.

3. Google Account Authorization

Google authentication and authorization are handled by Google. Super Converter Pro does not ask users to provide their Google account password to the App.

The App requests the Google Drive permission required for its Drive upload feature. The intended Drive scope is:

https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file

This permission is intended to provide access to files created or used by the application rather than unrestricted access to the user's entire Google Drive.

4. Information We Receive

Super Converter Pro is primarily a static web application. We do not operate a backend database intended to store users' uploaded documents.

When the user explicitly chooses Google Drive upload, the selected output file is transmitted to Google Drive through Google's Drive API.

5. Sharing and WhatsApp

If a user chooses the device's Share or WhatsApp functionality, the file is handled by the browser's operating-system sharing mechanism and the third-party application selected by the user.

Third-party services such as WhatsApp have their own privacy policies and terms of service.

6. Cookies and Analytics

Super Converter Pro does not intentionally sell personal information or use user documents for advertising purposes.

The browser, Google services, hosting services, or other third-party libraries used by the App may use technical storage, cookies or similar technologies according to their own policies.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Files processed locally by the App remain under the user's control on their device unless the user chooses to share or upload them elsewhere.

Files uploaded to Google Drive are stored in the user's Google Drive account and can be deleted by the user using Google's normal Drive controls.

8. Third-Party Services

Some features may use third-party browser libraries, Google services, OCR libraries, document-processing libraries or sharing applications.

These third-party services may have their own privacy policies, terms and data practices.

9. Security

We aim to minimize unnecessary server-side handling of user documents. Supported browser-based processing is designed to take place locally in the user's browser.

However, no website or Internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Children's Privacy

The App is not specifically directed at children. Users should comply with applicable laws and the requirements of any third-party services they use with the App.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the App's features or data practices change. The latest version will always be published on this page.
